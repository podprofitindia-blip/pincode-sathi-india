# NESTORA V2 — Affiliate Website

Premium NESTORA homepage with a starter affiliate catalog for home storage & organization.

## Before publishing
1. Open `script.js` and replace `YOUR-TAG-21` with your approved Amazon Associates tracking ID.
2. Verify each product, price and availability on Amazon before publishing.
3. Replace demo product symbols with product images obtained through permitted affiliate assets/tools.
4. Add real Privacy Policy, Terms, Disclaimer and Affiliate Disclosure pages.
5. Add a real contact email and verify the NESTORA domain/trademark/social handles.

The site does not process payments or orders; product links send visitors to the retailer.
